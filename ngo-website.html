Zero Hunger Initiative

No one should have to sleep hungry.

We believe food is a basic human right. Ektamission works tirelessly to provide hot, nutritious meals to patients' families in hospitals and underprivileged communities across the region.

Our Vision

A society completely free from hunger.

We imagine a world where financial struggle never means an empty stomach, especially for families caring for their sick loved ones in hospitals.

Our Mission

Bringing fresh meals to the last mile.

We cook and deliver thousands of fresh, hygienic meals daily, ensuring that those going through tough medical times don't have to worry about their next meal.

What We Do

How we are fighting hunger every day.

01

Hospital Langar Service

Family members of patients often stay outside hospitals for days without proper food. We bridge this gap by distributing fresh, hot meals across 60+ hospitals directly to those in need.

02

Community Food Drives

Beyond hospitals, our volunteers reach out to the streets and underprivileged areas, ensuring that the most vulnerable people get a dignified and healthy meal.
